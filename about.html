bio.md

I'm a full-stack developer with over 3 years of experience building and maintaining efficient, scalable web applications and internal business systems for government and academic institutions, with additional background in technical support and multimedia production.

Day to day, that means developing standalone applications and web-based systems, administering databases across departments, managing network security for internal systems, and troubleshooting hardware, software, and network issues for staff. I've also operated drones (UAVs) for aerial fieldwork data collection in support of agency projects.

Recently I expanded into AI-assisted animated content production — directing an end-to-end pipeline from character and asset generation through final video output for independently produced animated shorts, applying the same systems-thinking and technical rigor to a creative pipeline.

I handle my own hosting, deployment, and client communication on freelance projects, and I'm comfortable working with modern AI tools for both development (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT) and content generation (Veo 3.1, ElevenLabs, Google Flow, HiggsField, KlingAI, Suno AI).
