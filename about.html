About

Full-stack developer, based in the Philippines, working independently with clients across freelance platforms. I build systems more often than interfaces — the kind of software that runs quietly in the background, tracking, managing, or reporting on something that matters to the people using it.

bio.md

I'm a mid-level full-stack developer with 3–5 years of experience, working primarily with PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, Node.js, React/Next.js, and WordPress. Most of my client work comes through freelance platforms like OnlineJobs.ph and Contra, where I take on projects independently rather than as part of an agency.

I'd rather build the system than design the screen. Given the choice, I gravitate toward application and system development over UI/UX work — figuring out how data should move, how a process should be automated, how a dashboard should reflect what's actually happening. That preference shows up clearly in the government systems I've built for DENR: air and water quality monitoring, a recruitment/selection/placement platform, and an ICT equipment monitoring and inspection tracker.

On the client side, I've integrated CRM infrastructure like Flipping Pro (built on GoHighLevel) into custom WordPress sites, managed hosting and deployment through Hostinger, and more recently expanded into AI-assisted video and image production for clients who need content, not just code.

I handle my own hosting, deployment, and client communication — which means every project on this site went through my hands end to end, not just the parts I found interesting.
